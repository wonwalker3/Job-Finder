## B) Candidate match

### Strong alignment

- **Real estate ecosystem fit:** This is one of the strongest parts of the match. Qualia operates in the home buying / closing workflow, and Kody already understands the surrounding ecosystem: buyers, lenders, agents, transactions, timelines, and trust-heavy coordination.
- **Stakeholder coordination:** The role wants someone who can quarterback implementations and keep multiple groups aligned. Kody has years of coordinating buyers, agents, lenders, title, warranty, and construction stakeholders.
- **Customer-facing communication:** Strong match. The role calls for training, troubleshooting, roadmap communication, and managing ambiguity with senior stakeholders. That is very consistent with his background.
- **CRM / tool fluency:** The role prefers Salesforce or similar tools, and Kody has direct CRM experience across Salesforce, HubSpot, and Dynamics 365.
- **Operator mentality:** This role wants judgment, independence, and the ability to unblock progress. That fits well.

### Real gaps / risks

- **Direct implementation title gap:** Kody has not held a pure SaaS implementation role by title.
- **Title & escrow preference:** The JD explicitly says 3–5 years in a relevant customer success role, or title & escrow industry experience. Kody has strong adjacent real estate experience, but not literal title/escrow background.
- **Less direct revenue role:** Compared with AE roles, this sits more in onboarding and implementation than closing. That can still fit, but it is a different day-to-day motion.

### Gap severity

- Implementation-title gap: **manageable**
- Title/escrow preference gap: **real but not fatal**
- Shift from sales to onboarding/delivery: **manageable if interested**

## C) Positioning strategy

Lead with this angle:

> customer-facing real estate operator who already understands the transaction ecosystem, knows how to keep multiple parties aligned, and can guide customers through a structured rollout with credibility and urgency.

### What to emphasize

- Real estate transaction familiarity
- Multi-stakeholder coordination
- Customer communication and expectation-setting
- Troubleshooting and driving momentum when deals/processes get messy
- CRM/tool fluency and process discipline
- Ability to explain value and ROI in business terms

### What to de-emphasize

- Pure closing/commission language that makes the role feel like a downgrade from sales
- Overly technical infrastructure talk
- Anything that suggests he expects the job to be quota-carrying AE work

### Best story

This is a good role if framed as:
- not “software implementation expert from birth”
- but “someone who deeply understands the customer world and can own complex customer motion from kickoff through successful transition”

That’s believable.
