- **Full-cycle sales fit:** Kody clearly matches the need for someone who can run discovery, qualification, negotiation, and closing.
- **Volume and pipeline fit:** SMB AE work demands pace and pipeline discipline. Kody's history managing large active pipelines and many concurrent deals transfers well.
- **CRM fit:** Salesforce, HubSpot, and Dynamics 365 experience all help here.
- **Consultative fit:** The role calls for consultative selling and customer understanding. That is already one of Kody's strongest transferable muscles.
- **AI / systems angle:** HubSpot is AI-forward, and Kody's practical AI use gives him a more modern story than a lot of traditional sellers.

### Real gaps / risks

- **Pure SaaS motion gap:** This is a more obvious SaaS benchmark role than Luxury Presence. The “have you carried a software quota before?” question will come up harder here.
- **SMB net-new logo environment:** Depending on the exact motion, this may involve faster cadence, more outbound, and less relationship carryover than Kody's strongest historic lane.
- **Brand-bar / process bar:** HubSpot is a polished operator. They may screen harder for traditional SaaS indicators than a domain-fit company would.

### Gap severity

- SaaS-title gap: **real but manageable**
- SMB velocity adjustment: **manageable**
- Credential / background filtering risk: **real**

## C) Positioning strategy

Lead with this angle:

> experienced full-cycle seller with real pipeline discipline, high-volume deal management, and strong consultative instincts who already uses modern tooling and AI to improve speed, quality, and follow-through.

### What to emphasize

- Full-cycle selling in trust-heavy environments
- Large active pipeline ownership
- Forecasting and CRM hygiene
- Ability to manage many deals at once without losing the plot
- Practical use of AI and workflow systems
- Coachability and speed of learning in new tools/processes

### What to de-emphasize

- Anything too rooted in homebuilding specifics unless it clearly translates to sales process skill
- Overly niche real-estate detail that distracts from general commercial selling ability
- Technical infrastructure detail beyond what supports the “modern operator” story

### Best sales story

This is not “I sold houses, so I can sell anything.”
It works better as:
- I have already run disciplined full-cycle pipelines
- I know how to manage high activity volume
- I know how to sell consultatively
- I already use CRM and AI tooling like someone who belongs in a modern GTM org
- now I am applying that in a software-driven environment
