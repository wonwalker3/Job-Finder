## A) Role snapshot

- **What they are really buying:** a consultative seller who can own prospecting, discovery, solutioning, proposals, negotiation, and close while using CRM and AI workflows intelligently

## B) Candidate match

- Full-cycle sales fit is clear
- Consultative selling fit is clear

- Mix of outbound + warm inbound is realistic
- Startup/dynamic environment fit is good

### Gap severity

- domain gap: manageable
- overall fit: solid secondary target

## C) Positioning strategy
Lead with: full-cycle consultative seller who already uses AI/tools in practical ways and can help clients translate workforce or capacity pain into business outcomes.

## D) Compensation / market read
- Role quality: good
- Fit quality: strong secondary
- Better than prestige-bait SaaS roles because the JD values AI workflow fluency and consultative process, not just logo pedigree.

- Emphasize full-cycle selling, consultative discovery, proposals/negotiation, CRM + AI workflow fluency
- Keep operator/business judgment tone
