# Evaluation: Glean — Commercial Solutions Engineer

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Archetype:** AI-forward commercial solutions engineering role  
**Score:** 2.9/5

## A) Role snapshot

- **Company:** Glean
- **Title:** Commercial Solutions Engineer
- **Location:** Remote
- **Comp:** $110K–$215K base, plus variable/equity potential
- **Role type:** customer-facing technical pre/post-sales role supporting mid-market AI platform deals
- **What they are really buying:** a true solutions engineer who can partner with AEs, run technical discovery, build demos, manage technical POCs, handle security conversations, and work credibly across APIs, integrations, cloud environments, and code-adjacent workflows

## B) Candidate match

### Strong alignment

- **Consultative customer motion:** Kody does fit the customer-facing, consultative, multi-stakeholder side of this role.
- **AI-forward comfort:** He has a real AI/operator story, not a fake one.
- **Communication and business translation:** He can likely explain value, drive conversations, and keep customer-facing momentum well.
- **Commercial partnership:** Working alongside AEs and helping move deals forward is directionally aligned with his strengths.

### Real gaps / risks

- **Direct solutions engineering requirement:** The JD explicitly asks for 5+ years of sales engineering or solution consulting in a SaaS organization.
- **Technical bar:** It also asks for cloud familiarity, Python or Java, API-based integrations, technical POCs, and security-objection handling. That is a real requirement, not decoration.
- **Education signal:** Bachelor’s degree in CS or related field is listed.
- **Enterprise technical deal-cycle depth:** The role expects proven experience partnering with AEs on technical enterprise cycles.

### Gap severity

- Solutions-engineering title gap: **serious**
- Technical depth gap: **serious**
- Commercial/customer-facing overlap: **real but insufficient**

## C) Positioning strategy

If forced to make the case, the best angle is:

> strong customer-facing commercial operator with real AI fluency and strong communication skills who could support customer conversations on a modern AI platform.

But that still falls short of what Glean is actually hiring for.

### What to emphasize

- customer-facing confidence
- consultative conversation skill
- ability to understand business use cases and explain ROI
- practical AI adoption and operator mindset

### What to de-emphasize

- pretending to have deep POC / integration / cloud / API experience
- implying true sales-engineering pedigree that is not there
- framing this as a clean fit just because the company is AI-forward

### Best honest story

This is interesting adjacency, not a strong target. The commercial instincts are relevant; the technical solutions-engineering bar is the problem.

## D) Compensation / market read

- **Company quality:** high
- **Role appeal:** high on paper
- **Reality:** likely too technical for the current profile to be competitive

This is exactly the kind of role that looks seductive because it sits near AI + customer-facing work, but the actual hiring bar is for a solutions engineer, not a commercial operator with tooling curiosity.

## E) Resume tailoring plan

### Summary changes if applying anyway

- lead with consultative customer work and AI comfort

- stress business translation rather than code/integration depth

### Experience bullet priorities
