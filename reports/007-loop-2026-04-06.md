- **Comp:** ~$98.8K base + ~$98.8K commission target

## B) Candidate match

### Strong alignment

- **Full-cycle sales fit:** Clear match. Kody can credibly speak to discovery, qualification, negotiation, close, and handoff.
- **Pipeline / forecasting fit:** Loop explicitly values revenue forecasting, CRM hygiene, and data-driven selling. Kody has good evidence here.
- **Consultative selling fit:** They want curiosity, business-model understanding, and consultative pitching. That maps well to his strongest commercial instincts.
- **Multi-stakeholder fit:** The role involves customer experience, technical, product, and accounting stakeholders. Kody is already comfortable running layered deals with many parties.
- **Startup-ish ambiguity:** Loop values ownership and shaping the playbook. That’s a positive fit.

### Real gaps / risks

- **Direct SaaS tenure:** They want 2+ years of SaaS sales experience. This is lighter than some roles, but it still matters.
- **Ecommerce / Shopify context:** Helpful but not central in Kody’s background.
- **Expansion / cross-sell inside multi-product SaaS:** Directionally believable, but less directly proven than in his cleaner domain-fit roles.

### Gap severity

- SaaS-title gap: **manageable**
- Ecommerce context gap: **manageable**
- Mid-market SaaS polish expectation: **real but not fatal**

### What to emphasize

- full-cycle selling and quota discipline
- clean CRM / forecast hygiene
- multi-stakeholder sales motion

### What to de-emphasize

- over-rotating into technical product detail
- sounding like a generic SaaS AE clone

- it’s consultative
- it values ownership and process
- the bar is real, but not absurdly pedigree-driven
