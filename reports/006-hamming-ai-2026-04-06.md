## A) Role snapshot

- **Company:** Hamming AI
- **Title:** Account Executive
- **Location:** Austin / hybrid (also says remote North America in listing)
- **Role type:** full-cycle AE role selling an AI infrastructure / QA product for voice agents
- **What they are really buying:** an operator-grade closer who can prospect, run discovery, manage technical evaluations, navigate procurement/security, and close multi-threaded deals with engineering/product/AI stakeholders

## B) Candidate match

### Strong alignment

- **Full-cycle commercial fit:** The role is still fundamentally an AE role. Kody has the discovery, qualification, pipeline, negotiation, and close motion.
- **AI-forward story:** Unlike many generic SaaS roles, this company will actually care that he uses AI in real workflows and can speak credibly about automation.
- **High-agency environment:** Startup speed, ambiguity, and founder proximity are all compatible with his operator style.
- **Multi-stakeholder fit:** They want someone who can run complex sales with engineering, product, AI/ML, and security stakeholders in the mix. Kody already has strong experience navigating layered stakeholder environments, even if not in identical categories.
- **Austin advantage:** Local/hybrid alignment is a plus.

### Real gaps / risks

- **Direct B2B SaaS closing requirement:** They explicitly ask for 3+ years of quota-carrying closing experience in B2B SaaS.
- **Technical stakeholder depth:** This is still a more technical sale than most of Kody's cleanest-fit roles.
- **Developer / AI infrastructure context:** Bonus points include voice AI stacks, infra, testing, observability, security/compliance, etc. That is not his native lane.
- **Startup sharpness bar:** They sound intense and likely expect high precision and very fast ramp.

### Gap severity

- SaaS-title gap: **real but manageable**
- Technical sale complexity: **moderate**
- Deep category expertise gap: **real but not fatal if the commercial motion is strong**

## C) Positioning strategy

Lead with this angle:

> full-cycle closer with real AI fluency, strong discovery instincts, and high operational discipline who can sell a technical product by translating it into business outcomes and managing complex stakeholder motion.

### What to emphasize

- disciplined full-cycle sales motion
- high-agency execution
- comfort working in ambiguity and moving fast
- AI/automation usage that is practical, not cosmetic
- ability to run trust-heavy, multi-threaded buying processes
- clean CRM / forecast / next-step discipline

### What to de-emphasize

- pretending to be a developer-tools seller already
- acting like voice AI/infra/testing is a native background
- generic “I love AI” fluff

### Best story

This is not a domain-fit layup like Luxury Presence.
But it is a believable stretch if framed as:
- commercially strong
- AI-literate
- comfortable with technical buyers
- able to learn fast in a founder-led environment

That’s a better story than trying to force a true solutions-engineering role.
