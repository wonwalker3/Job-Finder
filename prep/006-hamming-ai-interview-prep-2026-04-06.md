# Interview Prep — Hamming AI / Account Executive

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Priority:** High  
**Source report:** `reports/006-hamming-ai-2026-04-06.md`

## Core positioning

This is not “I’m a voice AI expert.”
It is:

> I’m a disciplined full-cycle seller who already uses AI in real workflows, can talk to technical stakeholders without getting intimidated, and can keep complex deals moving.

## 30-second intro

"I’ve spent the last 13+ years in full-cycle consultative sales, owning everything from first conversation through close, often in complex multi-stakeholder environments. What makes Hamming interesting to me is that it’s still fundamentally a commercial role, but in a category where AI and product reliability actually matter. I already use AI daily in practical ways, and I’m comfortable learning quickly, translating technical value into business outcomes, and driving disciplined deal motion."

## What to emphasize
- full-cycle sales discipline
- technical stakeholder comfort
- AI usage that is real and practical
- startup/founder environment fit
- ability to quantify pain and business value

## Likely questions
1. Why Hamming AI?
2. How do you sell to technical stakeholders?
3. How would you handle a security/procurement-heavy cycle?
4. What’s your experience with AI products?
5. Why are you a fit without classic SaaS pedigree?

## Good answer to the SaaS-pedigree question

"I’m not going to pretend I’ve had the exact title path you see on a perfect SaaS resume. What I do have is the actual commercial motion: discovery, qualification, stakeholder alignment, negotiation, and close. The reason this role still makes sense is that I’m not starting from zero on AI or technical conversations — I use these tools in real workflows already, and I’m strong at translating value into business terms."

## Questions to ask them
- What makes the best AE successful at Hamming in the first 60–90 days?
- How technical do your best commercial conversations actually get?
- What usually stalls deals: technical validation, procurement, or urgency?
- How much founder involvement is there in the sales process?
