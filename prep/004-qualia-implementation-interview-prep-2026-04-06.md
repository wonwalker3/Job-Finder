# Interview Prep — Qualia / Implementation Manager, Mid-Market

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Priority:** High  
**Source report:** `reports/004-qualia-2026-04-06.md`

## Core positioning

You are not trying to sound like a career SaaS implementation consultant.
You are selling this:

> I already understand the customer world, I know how to keep complicated transaction workflows moving, and I can guide multiple stakeholders through process-heavy rollouts without losing trust or momentum.

## 30-second intro

"My background is in customer-facing real-estate sales and operations, where I’ve spent years managing high-stakes, multi-party workflows involving buyers, agents, lenders, and internal teams. What makes Qualia compelling is that it serves a customer world I already understand, but from the software and implementation side. I’m used to keeping complicated processes organized, communicating clearly, and helping people move through time-sensitive workflows without things falling apart."

## Best themes to hit
- real-estate ecosystem familiarity
- stakeholder alignment
- process ownership
- customer communication under pressure
- troubleshooting and unblocking progress
- CRM/system discipline

## Likely questions
1. Why implementation vs pure sales?
2. How do you handle blockers during a rollout?
3. How do you manage multiple stakeholders with competing priorities?
4. What do you know about title/escrow and transaction workflows?
5. How do you keep customers confident when the process gets messy?

## Good answer to “Why implementation?”

"A lot of the work I’ve done already sits in that zone. I’ve spent years helping people through complicated, time-sensitive processes where communication, organization, trust, and follow-through matter just as much as the initial sale. That’s part of the work I genuinely like."

## Questions to ask them
- What tends to derail implementations most often?
- How do your best implementation managers create trust early?
- What does success look like in the first 60–90 days?
- How much of the role is training, troubleshooting, and project coordination versus account growth?
