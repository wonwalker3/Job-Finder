# Interview Prep — Qualia / Customer Success Manager, Enterprise

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Priority:** High  
**Source report:** `reports/011-qualia-enterprise-csm-2026-04-06.md`

## Core positioning

This should sound like:

> a customer-facing real-estate operator who already understands the ecosystem, can build trust with strategic accounts, and knows how to keep complicated workflows and relationships healthy over time.

## 30-second intro

"My background is in full-cycle, customer-facing real-estate sales and operations, where I’ve spent years managing high-stakes, multi-party workflows and building trust across agents, buyers, lenders, and internal teams. What makes this role stand out to me is that it focuses on long-term customer value in a market I already understand well. I’m very comfortable owning relationships, keeping process-heavy work moving, and helping customers feel supported and confident through complex situations."

## Best themes to hit
- strategic relationship ownership
- real-estate ecosystem familiarity
- stakeholder coordination
- account growth / trust / retention
- process discipline
- calm communication under pressure

## Likely questions
1. Why enterprise customer success?
2. How do you build trust with larger accounts?
3. How do you handle escalations or friction?
4. What does customer growth look like without making the relationship feel transactional?
5. What experience do you have with title/escrow or transaction-heavy customer workflows?

## Good answer to “Why enterprise customer success?”

"Because I like the part of the work that happens after the initial excitement — building the relationship, keeping momentum, solving issues, and helping the customer actually get value over time. In my background, that kind of trust-building and process leadership has mattered a lot."

## Questions to ask them
- What does a great enterprise CSM at Qualia do better than an average one?
- Where do enterprise accounts typically feel the most friction today?
- How do you balance strategic account growth with day-to-day customer support needs?
- What does success look like in the first 90 days?
