# Interview Prep — Upwork / Marketplace Account Executive

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Priority:** High  
**Source report:** `reports/015-upwork-2026-04-06.md`

## Core positioning

This is a business-outcomes selling story.

> I’m a full-cycle consultative seller who already uses AI and CRM workflows in practical ways, and I can help customers connect workforce or capacity pain to a clear business solution.

## 30-second intro

"I’ve spent the last 13+ years in full-cycle consultative sales, managing pipeline, discovery, negotiation, and close in multi-stakeholder environments. What stands out to me about Upwork is that the role sits at the intersection of commercial execution and the future of how work gets done. I already use AI and workflow tools in practical ways, and I’m comfortable helping customers translate business pain into a clear path forward."

## Best themes to hit
- consultative selling
- prospecting + discovery + proposals + close
- CRM and AI workflow fluency
- business problem solving
- high-agency execution

## Likely questions
1. Why Upwork?
2. How do you discover business pain effectively?
3. How do you balance outbound with warm inbound?
4. How do you use AI in your workflow without losing quality?
5. What makes you credible in a marketplace/workforce story?

## Good answer to “Why Upwork?”

"Because it’s one of the few companies where the AI and workflow angle actually connects to a practical business problem. I’m not interested in AI as branding. I’m interested in tools that help companies move faster and solve capacity problems in the real world, and that makes this role feel more substantive than a generic AE seat."

## Questions to ask them
- What separates top performers from average ones here?
- How much of pipeline is self-created versus platform/marketing generated?
- What are the most common customer objections in the first conversation?
- Where do deals usually get stuck?
