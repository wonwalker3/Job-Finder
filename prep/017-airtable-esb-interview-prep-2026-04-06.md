# Interview Prep — Airtable / Account Executive, Emerging Small Business

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Priority:** High  
**Source report:** `reports/017-airtable-esb-2026-04-06.md`

## Core positioning

This is not a prestige-SaaS story.
It’s a volume + discipline + commercial execution story.

> I know how to manage a large active book, move quickly, keep CRM clean, protect revenue, and find growth inside a lot of moving opportunities.

## 30-second intro

"I’ve spent the last 13+ years in full-cycle sales, managing active pipelines, coordinating multiple stakeholders, and keeping process discipline strong in fast-moving environments. What makes this role appealing is that it rewards responsiveness, organization, expansion thinking, and clean commercial execution — all areas where I’ve already built strong habits. I also like that Airtable solves practical workflow problems for businesses, which makes the product easier to believe in and sell."

## Best themes to hit
- managing lots of moving opportunities at once
- responsiveness and follow-through
- CRM hygiene and forecasting
- expansion/growth mindset
- customer communication
- operator discipline

## Likely questions
1. How do you manage a large book of business?
2. How do you prioritize when many accounts need attention at once?
3. How do you think about renewals vs expansion vs net-new?
4. What makes you a fit for SMB/ESB motion?
5. Why Airtable?

## Good answer to “How do you manage a large book?”

"I like simple discipline: clear next steps, fast follow-up, honest prioritization, and clean systems. When you have a lot of active opportunities, sloppiness compounds quickly, so I try to keep visibility high and friction low."

## Questions to ask them
- What does a great first 90 days look like in this role?
- How much of the motion is true expansion versus net-new?
- What tends to separate top performers from average ones on this team?
- Where do deals or renewals most often get stuck?
