# Interview Prep — Qualia / Customer Success Manager, SMB

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Priority:** High  
**Source report:** `reports/012-qualia-smb-csm-2026-04-06.md`

## Core positioning

You are not trying to sound like a generic SaaS CSM.
You are selling this idea:

> I already understand the customer world Qualia serves, I’ve spent years guiding people through high-stakes real-estate workflows, and I know how to keep trust, communication, and momentum intact when a process gets messy.

## 30-second intro

"My background is in full-cycle sales and customer-facing real-estate operations, where I’ve spent years managing long-cycle, multi-stakeholder transactions with buyers, agents, lenders, and internal teams. What makes Qualia interesting to me is that it serves a customer world I already understand well, but from the software and workflow side. That feels like a very believable fit because I’m used to keeping complicated customer processes moving and creating trust along the way."

## Best themes to hit
- real-estate ecosystem familiarity
- stakeholder coordination
- customer communication under pressure
- keeping process momentum
- relationship-driven account growth
- CRM/system discipline

## Likely questions
1. Why customer success instead of pure sales?
2. How do you manage difficult customers or process friction?
3. How do you grow account value without damaging trust?
4. What do you know about title / escrow / closing workflows?
5. How do you keep multiple accounts organized?

## Good answer to “Why customer success?”

"Because a lot of the work I’ve done already lives in that zone — managing expectations, guiding people through complicated processes, keeping momentum, and creating confidence during stressful transactions. I like the relationship side of the work, not just the close."

## Questions to ask them
- What does success in the first 90 days actually look like?
- Where do SMB customers struggle most after onboarding?
- How much of the role is reactive support vs proactive growth?
- What separates your best CSMs from average ones?
